Here's a breakdown of the Feynman Technique:
1. Select a topic:
Choose a concept you want to learn. 
2. Explain it to yourself (or a child):
Write down everything you know about the topic in simple, clear language, as if you were explaining it to someone who knows nothing about it. 
3. Identify knowledge gaps:
Compare your explanation to your original source material (textbooks, notes, etc.) to identify areas where your understanding is weak or incomplete. 
4. Review and refine:
Fill in the knowledge gaps by revisiting your sources and re-explaining the concepts in a simpler, more refined way, using analogies or diagrams if necessary. 
5. Test and practice:
Use your simplified explanation to test your understanding and practice explaining it to others. 
The Feynman Technique: How to Learn Anything Quickly
By actively engaging with the material and identifying areas where you need to learn more, the Feynman Technique helps you build a deeper and more lasting understanding of complex topics.
